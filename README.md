# 👋 Hi, I'm Martin

I'm a software engineer at **RockLogic GmbH**, an Austria-based Ethereum node operator in the **Lido Curated Set** and the team behind **Stereum**. My day-to-day lives at the intersection of Ethereum staking infrastructure and DevOps, built on roots in low-level and embedded systems.

## 🚀 What I'm Working On

- 🧱 **Stereum & StereumLabs**  
  I help build and maintain [Stereum](https://stereum.com), the open-source tool for setting up and monitoring Ethereum nodes, and work on [StereumLabs](https://stereumlabs.com). On the side I'm developing a lightweight take on Stereum, focused on simplicity, performance, and managing multiple nodes more efficiently.

- ⛓️ **Validator Operations**  
  Running and keeping Ethereum validators healthy, and doing the reliability work that keeps stake online through hard forks and everything in between.

- 🎓 **Computer Science Degree @ TU Wien**  
  Probably the most time-consuming thing in my life right now is working toward my degree at TU Wien.

## 🛠️ Tech I Work With

- **Infra & DevOps:** Ansible is my strong suit, alongside Docker, GitHub Actions, nginx, systemd, and Linux/Ubuntu administration & networking.
- **Monitoring & observability:** Prometheus, Grafana and exporters like node_exporter are my daily drivers, with some ELK stack in the mix.
- **Ethereum clients:** Comfortable across the full range of consensus and execution clients, from Lighthouse, Teku, Prysm, Nimbus, Lodestar and Grandine to Geth, Nethermind, Besu, Reth, Erigon and Ethrex.
- **Languages:** Python and JavaScript/Node.js day to day. Go powers a small project of mine, and I picked up Java through my CS coursework at TU Wien.
- **Roots:** embedded and low-level programming, as below.

## 🧰 Background

Before diving deep into Ethereum and distributed systems, I studied **Electronics and Technical Computer Science / Informatics** at **HTL Hollabrunn**, where I built a strong foundation in **embedded systems development** and low-level programming.  
From blinking LEDs to Ethereum nodes: what a journey it's been, and there's so much more ahead.
